About and method

This dashboard supports research on how the boards of major technology companies oversee forced labour risks in their mineral supply chains, and how far that oversight aligns with the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights on leverage, remedy and responsible exit. The pilot covers cobalt at Apple, Microsoft and Tesla.

  • Exposure: traced upstream in Sayari from anchor suppliers each company names itself (cobalt HS codes 2605, 2822, 8105; trade records since 1 Jan 2023; depth 3). Colour and shape show the risk context; badges show evidence strength (E1 official list, E2 named in research, E3 network link, E4 product–country risk).
  • Severity-and-leverage test: state-imposed forced labour (little buyer leverage) points toward responsible exit; privately imposed forced labour (leverage available) points toward remedy.
  • Oversight: board coding uses the World Benchmarking Alliance CHRB board-level accountability indicators (A.2) plus seven project indicators, scored 0–2 from proxy statements. Coding is provisional and by a single coder.
  • Stewardship: related shareholder proposals 2022–2026 and Big Three holdings from SEC Form 13F. Fund-level votes (Form N-PX) are not yet collected.
  • Limitations: trade data miss artisanal and unrecorded flows; entity matching can be wrong; companies that disclose more can look more exposed; board deliberations are confidential.
