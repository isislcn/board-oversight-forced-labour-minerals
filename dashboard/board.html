Litigation, legal complaints and company actions

  • Litigation — concluded (all three): John Doe I et al. v. Apple, Alphabet, Dell, Microsoft and Tesla — forced child labour claims over DRC cobalt; filed Dec 2019, dismissed Nov 2021, dismissal affirmed by the D.C. Circuit on 5 Mar 2024. IRAdvocates · BHRRC
  • Criminal complaints — concluded in France, status unknown in Belgium (Apple): DRC government complaints over conflict minerals (3TG), filed Dec 2024; the Paris prosecutor closed the case in Feb 2025. Peters & Peters · Reuters
  • Apple — recycling: committed in Apr 2023 to 100% recycled cobalt in Apple-designed batteries by 2025; reports the target met at end-2025. Apple Newsroom, Apr 2026
  • Apple — regional exit (3TG): told suppliers in 2024 that their smelters and refiners must stop buying tin, tantalum, tungsten and gold from the DRC and Rwanda (statement to Reuters, Dec 2024). Reuters
  • Microsoft — engagement: requires cobalt reporting via RMI EMRT since 2019 and states it sources from conflict-affected and high-risk areas rather than avoiding them. Microsoft Conflict Minerals Report 2025
  • Tesla — sourcing agreements: cobalt supply agreement with Glencore reported in Jun 2020 (CNBC, industry sources); Huayou precursor supply Jul 2022 to end-2025 (Bloomberg, 2022). CNBC
